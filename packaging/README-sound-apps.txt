CAT SUITE - sound apps (Windows)
================================

The instruments that make their own sound, as standalone apps AND as VST3 plug-ins:
    Sing-Cat   - choir of cats and people
    Talk-Cat   - singer with lyrics (English, Slovak, Japanese, Cat)
    Glide-Cat  - gliding poly synth
    Life-Cat   - a tiny ecosystem that makes music
    Muse-Cat   - writes whole songs (or endless music) from a genre, a mood and a song number
    Cat Hub    - every Cat jamming together in one window; Dice and Compose write songs

How to install
1. Close Cubase and any open Cat apps.
2. Double-click "Install-Cat-Sound-Apps.bat".
3. Start the apps from the Start menu or the Desktop.

Playing with the computer keyboard (standalone apps)
    A S D F G H J K L      white keys (A = C3)
    W E T Y U O P          black keys (on a Slovak / German keyboard Z works as Y)
    Left / Right arrow     octave down / up
    Down / Up arrow        softer / harder
    If the keys stop playing, click the COMPUTER KEYBOARD strip at the bottom.

Cat Hub
    Press Play: the Hub walks through Chord-Cat's pads (one chord per bar), Bass-Cat
    plays a bass line to the chords, Drum-Cat plays the drums and Life-Cat's creatures
    sing along - all at the same tempo. No Cubase needed.
    Space                  play / stop
    1 - 8                  hold one of Chord-Cat's pads yourself
    Jam tab                sound, volume, room (reverb), mute / solo for each Cat
    Chord-Cat, Bass-Cat... tabs: each Cat's own window with all its settings
    Rec                    records what you hear to Music\Cat Hub\*.wav
    The Hub remembers everything (the Cats' settings too) for next time.

Sound card, MIDI keyboard: click "Options" (top left of the app) > Audio/MIDI Settings.

Where things go (no administrator rights needed)
    Apps:     C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Programs\Cat Suite
    VST3:     C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Programs\Common\VST3
