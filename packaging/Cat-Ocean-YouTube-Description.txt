🐟 CAT OCEAN - a relaxing Frutiger Aero pixel ocean that never ends 🫧
Get your own fish by typing in the chat! It swims in when you chat and naps in the cave when you're away (it's saved forever).
The music never stops: it is written live, and the fishes' genes slowly change its tempo and key. Every fish sings its own little tune.

Your fish earns XP while it swims (1 per minute) and when you chat (2, at most every 30 s).
* = unlocks with levels:  LV2 puffer, gradient, sail fin, sleepy eyes, scarf, chip voice

                          LV4 checker, veil tail, whistle voice   LV5 cat body, lamp   LV6 pad voice   LV8 crown

🧬 GENETICS
Fish have babies that mix their parents' colours, shapes and songs (your fish's babies are called "Miro Jr.").
Babies sometimes mutate - and a few are born Golden, Rainbow, Ghost, Glowing or Crystal.
After many generations evolution can discover a whole NEW SPECIES with something no fish had before.
